# Rehearse Defence

Delegate to `demo-defence-coach`.

1. Generate 12 questions across: why this approach over alternatives; accuracy/validity of results; failure modes and limits; data and third-party dependencies; security and privacy; scalability and deployment; what was cut and why; how each mandatory requirement is met.
2. Ask one at a time; the Founder answers in chat; the coach scores each answer 1-5 (rubric: Q&A defence anchors) and gives a tighter model answer drawing only on real artifacts.
3. Repeat weak areas until answers are specific, evidence-backed and under 45 seconds.
4. Save the final Q&A cheat sheet to `delivery/G7-release/evidence/qa-cheatsheet.md`.
